# 🗓️ DateNest
### Never Miss What Matters

> A personal life management app that automatically finds important dates
> from Gmail and Google Calendar - and reminds you early enough to act.

---

## 🎯 The Problem We Solved
From our survey of **43 real users**:
- **82%** have missed at least one important date before
- **41%** rely on memory alone - no system, no app
- People manage bills, insurance, birthdays, government deadlines
  across 5+ apps with zero connection between them

## 💡 What DateNest Does
DateNest connects to your Gmail and Google Calendar, finds your
important dates automatically, and reminds you through a smart
ladder — 30 days, 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before - with full
context in every notification.

---

## ✨ Core Features — MVP Phase 1
| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Gmail Auto-Scan | Finds LIC, HDFC, Star Health, MSEB dates automatically |
| Google Calendar Sync | Two-way sync — DateNest ↔ Calendar |
| Birthday Import | Pull from phone contacts, no typing needed |
| Reminder Ladder | 30/7/3/1 day alerts with amount + reference number |
| Home Dashboard | Next 7 days, colour-coded by urgency |
